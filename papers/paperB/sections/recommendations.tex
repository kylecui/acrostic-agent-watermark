\section{Recommendations}
\label{sec:recs}

\paragraph{For deployers.}
(1)~Calibrate in-domain, on $\ge$20 (production: $\ge$50) documents;
cross-domain calibration is worse than none (Finding~2).
(2)~Set a per-word vote cap and re-derive it when dictionary, language, or
codec mode changes; $c{=}3$ is the measured optimum for our configuration
(Finding~1). (3)~Route high-repetition texts (unique-sentence ratio below
$\sim$0.5) away from watermark verdicts to manual review (Finding~3).
(4)~Report existence and attribution conclusions with their calibration
domain, corpus size, and text-length band attached; never quote a bare
detection rate.

\paragraph{For researchers.}
(5)~Add the repetition construction and the template construction to the
standard attack suite; both are one line of code and neither appears in
current protocols. (6)~Report the statistic family ($z$-test vs.\
presence-ratio vs.\ regression) alongside robustness claims: our data show
the pitfall profile---F1/F2/F3 severity---is statistic-dependent, so
cross-scheme comparisons without this axis are uninterpretable.
(7)~Ship deterministic artifacts: PostMark's embedding table is a random
per-initialization subset, so its released scores are not bit-reproducible
across environments (our positive control was invalidated for this reason
and is reported as such). Fixed tables or published seeds are cheap
insurance.
