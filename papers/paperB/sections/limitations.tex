\section{Limitations and Ethics}

\paragraph{Scope.}
Our measurements use one host system (AAWM v0.15.0) end-to-end; generality
is supported by an independent reference implementation (Finding~1) and the
official PostMark implementation (Findings~2--3), but we do not claim
coverage of all watermark statistics. The cap value $c{=}3$ is validated
for the tested codec/dictionary configuration only. PostMark measurements
are null-side (existence/transfer); its embedding-pipeline reproduction was
invalidated by environment-level table nondeterminism and is reported as a
finding rather than silently dropped. Our corpora are public-domain texts
and constructed templates; a production audit should repeat the matrix on
in-house domains.

\paragraph{Ethics.}
Watermarks attribute documents to users; the same mechanism can trace
journalists' sources or whistleblowers. We support the position that
deployment-level watermarking of user-facing documents should be disclosed,
and that abstain-capable protocols with published failure modes---like the
ones characterized here---are preferable to silent ones.
